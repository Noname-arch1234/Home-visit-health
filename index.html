GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Is the appointment confirmed when I submit the form?＋

No. Your request is sent to our team. We will contact you to check doctor availability and confirm the appointment.

What is the consultation charge?＋

Consultation charges start at ₹1,200. The final amount, including any applicable travel charge, will be communicated before confirmation.

Do you cover all areas of Chennai?＋

We accept requests from all areas of Chennai. Each request is subject to doctor availability and service feasibility.

Can I request a specific doctor?＋

You may mention your preference in the WhatsApp conversation. Doctor assignment depends on availability and will be confirmed by our team.

!

Medical emergency? This home-visit service is not an emergency response service. For severe breathlessness, chest pain, stroke symptoms, major bleeding, unconsciousness or another medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.
